Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Duolo desktop app, the Duolo mobile apps and the Duolo service at duolo.io (together, "Duolo") handle, and why. Duolo is operated by Aita Technology (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. ("we").

The short version

  • Duolo runs coding agents on your own computer. Your code, your agent sessions and your task history stay on your computer.
  • The agents (such as Claude Code or Codex) talk to their own providers using your own accounts with those providers. That traffic does not go through us and is covered by those providers' privacy policies.
  • We only receive data when you use a feature that needs the Duolo service: voice input, signing in, or connecting your phone to your computer.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train models.

What we collect and why

Account

If you sign in, we store your email address and use it to send you one-time sign-in codes. If you use features that need the service without signing in, the app creates an anonymous identity so we can apply usage limits. We keep sign-in sessions for your devices, including the IP address and user agent recorded when a session is created, to keep your account secure.

Devices

For each device connected to your account we store its name, its platform (for example Mac or iOS), the public key used for end-to-end encryption and, if you allow notifications, a push token.

Messages between your phone and your computer

When your phone and your computer are paired, messages between them (task lists, conversations, commands) are end-to-end encrypted on the device before they reach us. We can't read them. We relay them, and if the receiving device is offline we hold the encrypted message for up to 7 days, then delete it.

Voice input

When you use voice input, the audio you record is streamed through our server to our speech recognition provider, Volcengine (ByteDance), together with hint words from the open task (such as file and function names) and recent lines of the conversation, so that technical terms are recognized correctly. If you ask Duolo to tidy up what you said, the transcript and hints are also sent to a language model provider. We don't store the audio or the transcript; we only record how many seconds of voice input each account used per day, to apply daily limits.

Update checks

The desktop app checks GitHub for new releases of Duolo and of the agents you installed. Those requests go directly to GitHub and are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

duolo.io doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page) for security and troubleshooting.

Where data is stored

The Duolo service runs on Alibaba Cloud servers in the United States. Voice audio is processed by Volcengine. These providers process data only on our behalf to run the service.

How long we keep it

  • Account and device information: until you delete your account or remove the device.
  • Encrypted relay messages: until delivered, at most 7 days.
  • Voice usage counts: up to 90 days.
  • Server access logs: up to 60 days.

Your choices and rights

You can use the desktop app without signing in and without voice input; then no personal data reaches us. You can remove a device from your account at any time. To get a copy of your data or to delete your account and all associated data, contact us as described below; we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Duolo is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/OtterMind/duolo/issues. For requests about your personal data, don't post the data itself in the issue; say what you need and we will contact you privately to verify and handle the request.
